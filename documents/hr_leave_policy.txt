ACME CORPORATION — LEAVE POLICY
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
=======================================================

1. OVERVIEW
------------
This policy outlines the types of leave available to all full-time employees
of ACME Corporation. Leave entitlements are pro-rated for part-time employees.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
----------------
Full-time employees are entitled to:
- 0–2 years of service: 14 days per year
- 2–5 years of service: 18 days per year
- 5+ years of service: 21 days per year

Annual leave must be applied for at least 5 working days in advance.
Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the
following calendar year. Leave balance exceeding this cap will be forfeited.

3. SICK LEAVE
--------------
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for absences
exceeding 2 consecutive days.

Hospitalisation leave is separate:
- Up to 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave per year (inclusive of
  the 14 days outpatient sick leave).

4. MATERNITY LEAVE
-------------------
Female employees who have been employed for at least 3 months are entitled to:
- 16 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children.
- 16 weeks of maternity leave (first 8 weeks government-paid, next 8 weeks
  employer-paid) from the third child onwards.

Notice must be given to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

5. PATERNITY LEAVE
-------------------
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave per birth.
Leave must be taken within 16 weeks of the child's birth.

6. CHILDCARE LEAVE
-------------------
Employees with children below 7 years of age are entitled to 6 days of
paid childcare leave per year.

Employees with children aged 7–12 are entitled to 2 days of extended
childcare leave per year.

7. COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
------------------------
Compassionate leave is granted for bereavement:
- Death of spouse, child, or parent: 3 days paid leave
- Death of sibling or parent-in-law: 2 days paid leave
- Death of other relatives: 1 day paid leave

8. MARRIAGE LEAVE
------------------
Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid marriage leave once during their
employment with the company. This must be taken within 1 month of the
solemnisation date.

9. STUDY AND EXAMINATION LEAVE
--------------------------------
Employees pursuing job-relevant qualifications approved by the company
are entitled to up to 5 days of paid study leave per year.
Examination dates must be provided to HR at least 2 weeks in advance.

10. UNPAID LEAVE
-----------------
Unpaid leave may be granted at the company's discretion for personal
reasons beyond the entitlements listed above. Applications must be
submitted in writing to HR and approved by the department head.

11. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
--------------------
Employees are entitled to all public holidays gazetted by the government.
If a public holiday falls on a weekend, a substitute day off will be granted
on the following Monday.

Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive:
- A replacement day off, OR
- 1.5x their daily rate as additional pay

12. HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
----------------------------
All leave applications must be submitted through the HR portal (hr.acmecorp.internal).
Approval from the line manager is required before leave is confirmed.
Last-minute sick leave must be notified to the line manager by 9:00 AM on
the day of absence, followed by a formal application in the HR portal.

13. LEAVE WITHOUT APPROVAL
----------------------------
Absence from work without prior approval or valid reason will be treated as
unauthorised absence and may result in disciplinary action.

END OF LEAVE POLICY
