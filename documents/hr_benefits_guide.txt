ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
Version 1.8 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
=======================================================

1. OVERVIEW
------------
ACME Corporation is committed to the well-being of its employees. This guide
summarises the benefits available to all confirmed full-time employees.
Benefits commence after the completion of the 3-month probation period.

2. HEALTH INSURANCE
--------------------
All confirmed employees and their immediate dependants (spouse and up to
2 children) are covered under the company's group health insurance plan.

Coverage includes:
- Inpatient hospitalisation and surgery
- Specialist outpatient consultations (with referral)
- General practitioner (GP) visits at panel clinics — fully covered
- Emergency treatment

Annual insurance limit:
- Employee: S$150,000
- Spouse: S$80,000
- Each child: S$50,000

Pre-existing conditions are subject to a 12-month waiting period.
The list of panel clinics and hospitals is available on the HR portal.

3. DENTAL BENEFITS
-------------------
Employees are entitled to S$500 per year for dental treatment.
Coverage includes routine cleaning, fillings, and basic extractions.
Cosmetic dental procedures (whitening, veneers, braces) are not covered.
Claims must be submitted with receipts within 3 months of treatment.

4. OPTICAL BENEFITS
--------------------
Employees receive S$300 per year for optical expenses, including:
- Prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses
- Eye examinations at registered optometrists

5. ANNUAL HEALTH SCREENING
----------------------------
All employees aged 35 and above are entitled to a company-subsidised
annual health screening at designated clinics.
Employees below 35 may opt in at a subsidised rate.

6. MENTAL WELLNESS PROGRAMME
------------------------------
ACME provides access to a confidential Employee Assistance Programme (EAP).
Services include:
- Up to 6 sessions per year with a licensed counsellor
- 24/7 mental health helpline
- Stress management and mindfulness workshops (quarterly)

All interactions are strictly confidential and not reported to management.

7. RETIREMENT AND CPF
-----------------------
CPF contributions are made in accordance with statutory requirements.
In addition, ACME contributes an additional 2% of the employee's monthly
basic salary to a supplementary retirement fund after 3 years of service.

8. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------------
Each employee receives an annual Learning & Development (L&D) budget:
- Junior staff (Grade 1–3): S$1,000 per year
- Senior staff (Grade 4–6): S$2,000 per year
- Managers and above: S$3,500 per year

Approved uses include:
- External courses, workshops, and certifications
- Conference attendance (subject to manager approval)
- Online learning platforms (Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, Udemy, etc.)

Employees who leave within 12 months of completing a company-sponsored
course may be required to reimburse the training cost on a pro-rated basis.

9. FLEXIBLE WORK ARRANGEMENTS
-------------------------------
Employees may apply for flexible work arrangements after completing
their probation period:

- Remote Work: Up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
- Flexi-hours: Start time may be shifted between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM,
  with corresponding end-time adjustment.

All arrangements must be formalised through HR and reviewed quarterly.

10. TRANSPORTATION ALLOWANCE
------------------------------
Employees who are required to travel for business purposes are reimbursed
for transportation at the following rates:
- Public transport: Actual cost (receipts required)
- Grab/taxi: Actual cost (receipts required, for work trips only)
- Personal car: S$0.70 per km

Monthly transport claims must be submitted by the 5th of the following month.

11. MEAL ALLOWANCE
-------------------
Employees working beyond 8:00 PM on weekdays are entitled to a meal
allowance of S$15 per day. Receipts must be submitted for reimbursement.

12. STAFF REFERRAL PROGRAMME
------------------------------
Employees who refer a candidate who is subsequently hired and completes
3 months of service will receive a referral bonus of S$1,000 (net of tax).

13. LONG SERVICE AWARDS
------------------------
- 5 years of service: S$500 cash + 2 extra days of leave
- 10 years of service: S$1,500 cash + 3 extra days of leave
- 15 years of service: S$3,000 cash + 5 extra days of leave + a personalised memento

14. STAFF RECREATION CLUB
---------------------------
All employees are automatically enrolled in the ACME Staff Recreation Club.
The club subsidises:
- Sports facilities and fitness classes
- Team-building events and annual retreats
- Festive celebrations and family days

15. HOW TO MAKE A CLAIM
-------------------------
All benefit claims must be submitted through the HR portal within
3 months of the expense date. Late claims will not be processed.

Required documents: original receipts, invoices, or letters from providers.
Processing time: 2–3 weeks for reimbursement via payroll.

For queries, contact: hr.benefits@acmecorp.com | Ext: 6800

END OF BENEFITS GUIDE
