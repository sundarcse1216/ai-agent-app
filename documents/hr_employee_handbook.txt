ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
=======================================================

1. WELCOME
----------
Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook outlines the company's policies,
expectations, and the benefits available to all employees. Please read it
carefully and keep a copy for your reference.

2. COMPANY VALUES
-----------------
- Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency in everything we do.
- Innovation: We encourage creative thinking and continuous improvement.
- Collaboration: We work together across teams to achieve shared goals.
- Respect: We treat every individual with dignity and fairness.

3. WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
-----------------------------
Standard office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees are expected to be punctual. Flexible working arrangements
(flexitime or remote work) must be approved by the direct manager.

Core hours when all employees must be available: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Repeated tardiness or unexplained absences will be subject to a formal
performance improvement process.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
-------------------
Employees must maintain professional behaviour at all times, both inside
and outside the workplace when representing the company.

Prohibited conduct includes:
- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
- Unauthorised disclosure of confidential company information
- Misuse of company assets, including IT systems
- Conflicts of interest that are not declared to HR

Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action,
up to and including termination.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
------------------------
Employees receive a formal performance review twice per year:
- Mid-Year Review: July
- Annual Review: December

Reviews assess competencies, goal achievement, and overall contribution.
Salary increments and promotions are linked to performance ratings.

6. DRESS CODE
--------------
Business casual attire is expected Monday through Thursday.
Casual dress is permitted on Fridays.
Client-facing events require business formal attire.

7. COMMUNICATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY
--------------------------------------
All company communications (email, Slack, etc.) on company devices
are subject to monitoring in accordance with applicable law.

Employees must not share proprietary information, client data, or
internal financials with unauthorised parties, during or after employment.

A Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is signed upon joining and remains
in effect for two years post-employment.

8. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
-------------------------
Step 1: Verbal Warning
Step 2: Written Warning
Step 3: Final Written Warning
Step 4: Termination

Serious misconduct (fraud, violence, gross negligence) may bypass
earlier steps and result in immediate dismissal.

9. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
------------------------
Employees who have a workplace grievance should:
1. Raise the issue informally with their line manager.
2. If unresolved, submit a formal grievance form to HR.
3. HR will investigate within 10 business days.
4. A written outcome will be provided to the employee.

10. HEALTH AND SAFETY
-----------------------
All employees are responsible for maintaining a safe working environment.
Emergency exits and first-aid kits are located on every floor.
Workplace accidents must be reported to HR and Facilities within 24 hours.

11. RESIGNATION AND OFFBOARDING
---------------------------------
The standard notice period is:
- Probation period: 1 week
- Up to 2 years of service: 1 month
- More than 2 years of service: 2 months

Exit interviews are conducted by HR on the last working day.
All company assets (laptop, access cards, etc.) must be returned.

END OF HANDBOOK
